We keep the same literal ordered relator pairs and 14 ac-r2-v1 moves, at unit cost. Uppercase letters denote inverses. The cap bounds the sum of both freely reduced word lengths at every state, including the endpoints. Let e join (xYYYYXyyy, yyxxYX) to (xYYYYXyyy, yyxxYYYYYXyyy). To test avoidance, delete both directions between these two states. Move ID 4 remains available elsewhere.
